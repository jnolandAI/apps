Bean Bye

Snap a photo of a 90s bean-bag plush and Bean Bye gives you a quick, funny value estimate, a roast, and what that same money would have done in the stock market. Keep a collection, see what it's (probably not) worth, and say goodbye with a farewell certificate.

Values are AI-generated estimates for entertainment, not professional appraisals. No account, no ads, no tracking.
