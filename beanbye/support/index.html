Common questions

How accurate are the values?

They are AI-generated estimates for fun, not appraisals. Real prices depend on tags, condition and actual recent sales. If you're thinking of selling, check completed listings on a marketplace first.

It identified the wrong plush.

Try a well-lit photo of the front, with the heart-shaped hang tag visible if it has one, or search by name instead.

It says the appraiser is on a break, or I'm going too fast.

To keep the service running for everyone there is a limit on how many identifications each device can do per hour and per day, and a daily limit for the whole service. Wait a bit and try again.

Do I need an account?

No. Identification needs an internet connection; your collection is stored on your iPhone.

Is the leaderboard real?

No. The leaderboard and the regret ticker are parody and are labelled that way in the app. They don't show real people or real data.
