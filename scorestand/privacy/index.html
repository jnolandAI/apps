Scorestand Privacy Policy

Effective October 23, 2026

Short version: Scorestand does not collect, transmit or sell any personal data. Everything you put into the app stays on your device.

What the app stores, and where

Scorestand stores the following only on your iPad, inside the app's own storage:

  • PDF scores you import from the Files app (copies of the files you choose).
  • Your markings (pen, highlighter and eraser strokes), page crop and zoom settings, setlists, and the titles, composers, instruments and tags you enter.
  • Local backup files the app creates, and any backup archive you choose to export.

The app has no account, no sign-in and no server. It does not send your scores, markings or library to us or to anyone else.

What we collect

Nothing. Scorestand contains no analytics, advertising, tracking or crash-reporting software, and it makes no network requests of its own. It does not use your location, contacts, camera, microphone or photos.

Data you choose to share

If you export a backup or share a file, iOS shows its standard share sheet and you decide where the file goes (for example Files, AirDrop or Mail). What happens to it after that is governed by the destination you chose, not by Scorestand.

Apple

Apple handles App Store purchases, downloads and payments under Apple's Privacy Policy. If you have turned on "Share With App Developers" in iOS settings, Apple may give us anonymous crash and usage statistics. We use these only to fix problems.

iCloud and device backups

Like other app data, Scorestand's data may be included in your iCloud or computer backups of your device if you have turned these on. Those backups are managed by Apple and by you.

Children

Scorestand collects no data from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

Delete scores inside the app, or delete the app to remove all of its data from your device. Exported backup files you saved elsewhere must be deleted where you saved them.

Changes

If a future version changes how data is handled (for example, by adding an optional sync feature), we will update this policy and the App Store privacy label before that version is released.
