Common questions

How do I import a score?

Tap Import and choose a PDF from the Files app. Scorestand keeps its own copy, so moving or deleting the original afterwards doesn't affect the app.

Do I need an account or an internet connection?

No. Scorestand works fully offline and has no account.

How do I back up or move my library to a new iPad?

Go to Settings, export a backup, and save it to Files, AirDrop or another location you control. On the new iPad, install Scorestand and restore from that backup file. Scorestand doesn't sync between devices.

My page-turn pedal doesn't work.

Scorestand supports Bluetooth pedals that work in keyboard mode, sending arrow or page keys. Pair the pedal in iPadOS Settings > Bluetooth, put it in keyboard mode (see your pedal's manual), then open a score and press the pedal. If it still doesn't turn pages, email us the pedal's make and model.

A marking shows as not saved.

Scorestand shows save failures instead of hiding them. Keep the app open and try again. If the warning stays, export a backup and contact us.
