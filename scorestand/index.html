Scorestand

A PDF sheet-music reader for iPad, built for the stand: fast page turns by tap or Bluetooth pedal, Apple Pencil markings that are saved as you write, ordered setlists, and local backups you control.

Scorestand works fully offline. There is no account, no sign-in, no ads and no tracking.
